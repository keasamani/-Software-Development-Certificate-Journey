Our Story

At X-Press Upholstery, our journey began with a passion for craftsmanship and a commitment to quality. Founded in a small workshop, we initially focused on restoring furniture for friends and family.
As our reputation grew, so did our vision. Recognizing the need for stylish car interior décor, we expanded our services to transform vehicles, ensuring every ride is a blend of comfort and elegance. Each project, whether it’s a classic car makeover or a furniture refresh, is approached with creativity and care, reflecting our clients' unique styles. Today, X-Upholstery stands as a trusted name in the community, dedicated to delivering personalized solutions that enhance both homes and vehicles. Our story is one of passion, artistry, and a commitment to making every space beautiful and comfortable.
